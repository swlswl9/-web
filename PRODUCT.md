# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: lightweight static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, chosen because this is an empty project and the first delivery is a visual homepage prototype.

## Users

Potential creative clients and collaborators assessing a visual designer's work and whether to start a conversation.

## Product Purpose

An editable personal portfolio homepage that introduces the designer, makes selected work easy to discover, and creates a clear route to contact.

## Positioning

The portfolio opens with the supplied blue-and-white sticker artwork as a living piece of the interface, not as a generic project grid.

## Operating Context

Visitors commonly arrive on desktop from a shared link, then may continue to browse on a phone. The first viewport should establish an energetic creative point of view before the work detail pages are added.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Current scope: a homepage with a works section, a skills section, and an about section.
- The homepage works section presents Stitch, Pim & Pom, Miffy, Bottle, Hello Kitty, and Christmas as a full-width looping showcase. Each project opens its matching supplied case-study image; the Christmas detail page contains two supplied boards.
- The hero is a faithful, SVG-layered implementation of the supplied homepage template; it intentionally has no video background.
- Personal name, email address, and social URLs are deliberately left as visible placeholders until supplied.

## Product Principles

1. The work remains the main character.
2. Motion should make the page feel tactile and alive without getting in the way of reading.
3. Scaffolding stays honest about what needs real portfolio content later.
4. Contact should always be easy to find.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Keyboard-accessible navigation and visible focus states.
- Respect visitors who request reduced motion.
- Maintain high contrast between blue, ink, and paper surfaces.
